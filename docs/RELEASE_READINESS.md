# Release Readiness and Manual QA

This project is **in active development**, not a signed-off release candidate. Passing GitHub Actions is necessary but not sufficient for all phases of the master specification.

## Automated acceptance

- `npm run typecheck` — strict TypeScript.
- `npm test` — deterministic physics/combat/progression tests.
- `npm run build` — deployable static Vite bundle.
- `npx playwright install chromium && npm run test:e2e` — browser gameplay, settings, biome renders, live boathouse crossing, game-over and retry.
- CI screenshot artifacts provide representative visuals but still require review.

## Manual playtesting required before claiming complete

- [ ] Complete **three uninterrupted 15-minute sessions** on Classic, Modern and Endless modes.
- [ ] Reach all five environments through natural gameplay and verify car/boat handoffs and collision fairness.
- [ ] Test truck call/docking success and failure, replenishment and safe deployment.
- [ ] Verify missiles against helicopters; oil, flaming wake and smoke against representative threats; civilian scoring lockout.
- [ ] Record 1080p average/1% low FPS on mainstream discrete and integrated GPUs, at each quality preset.
- [ ] Check WebGL context loss/recovery and memory trends after a 30-minute play session.
- [ ] Test Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari where WebGL2 support exists; keyboard and at least two gamepad layouts.
- [ ] Compare new scenic perspective, sculpted-vehicle and neon-city screenshots against real visual references; inspect road/terrain seams, model normals, reflections, shadows, contrast, speed readability and motion comfort; approve visual baselines.
- [ ] Check accessible keyboard navigation, key rebinding, reduced motion, high-contrast HUD and mute.
- [ ] Review third-party license notices, original art/audio provenance and product title legal clearance.
- [x] Generate and commit package-lock.json from npm registry through GitHub Actions.
- [ ] Confirm an end-to-end green npm ci run on GitHub Actions and retest from a fresh developer installation. The lockfile workflow is manual to prevent silent dependency changes.

## Known limitations

Road forks and branch selection, sophisticated boathouse interior transformation, advanced AI orchestration, richer authored audiovisual assets, full gamepad remapping, and reproducible hardware performance results remain work items. Consult [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md), not checkbox counts alone, for scope and status.

## Deployment

The production bundle is generated by `npm run build` in `dist/`. Deploy this directory to static HTTPS hosting with appropriate fallback and asset caching. Verify WebGL2 detection and music/audio autoplay restrictions on the target host. No production hosting endpoint is configured in this repository.

## Neon road reflection review (2026-10-21)

The new procedural wet-pavement pass is constrained to the neon biome and Medium/High presets. Playwright screenshots and shader compilation checks provide smoke coverage only. Before release, inspect wet-pavement alpha edges, reflective tint against actual city light sources, tile boundaries, road markings, bloom/exposure, low-preset exclusion, and GPU frame times at all quality levels.

## Vehicle dynamics art review (2026-10-21)

The fixed-step animation pass animates axle hubs and brake lighting without changing arcade vehicle physics. Check wheel roll direction and rate against on-road motion, steering direction, collision and knockback transitions, brake-tail brightness under restrained HDR bloom, shadow/contact behavior during body pitch, controller inputs, 15-minute session stability, and the Reduced Motion suspension-pitch suppression. Automated unit/Chromium smoke tests do not constitute hardware or artistic signoff.

## Aerial searchlight visual review (2026-10-21)

Verify that pale patrol and rose ace searchlights remain distinct from the **orange bomb impact warning**, that the ground pool tracks the curved highway rather than the flight model's raw X coordinate, and that Reduced Motion locks scanning and pulsing. Compare High, Medium and Low budgets in forest, snow and neon, including fog/bloom, terrain occlusion, long pursuit sessions, and early target destruction. The procedural cone is an art-directed transparent effect rather than physically correct volumetric illumination. Real-GPU frame-time and manual perception reviews remain required.

## Waterway wake fidelity review (2026-10-21)

Verify the new one-draw-call V-hull wake in High/Medium/Low quality, across centered and hard-steering river navigation. Check the pair of foam arms expand behind rather than ahead of the boat, do not interfere with projectile/barrel readability, align with the curved river surface, transition cleanly at the boathouse, and dissipate without growing draw calls or leaking objects in extended sessions. Compare foam tone and scale with water glints in camera captures; run actual integrated/dedicated GPU frame-time and long-session memory checks. Automated tests and a Chromium screenshot are smoke coverage, not visual signoff.

## Arcade driving and incoming threats validation (2026-10-21)

Verify holding W/↑ visibly drives the interceptor **up** toward the horizon, releasing the accelerator settles toward cruise, and holding S/↓ visibly drives it **down** without clipping the bottom HUD. Repeat at low/high aspect ratios and in boat mode. Under speed changes, confirm the actual player collision hitbox, bullets, missiles, rear hazards, oil/smoke, headlights, ski/skid tracks, foam wakes, bomb telegraphs and supply-van ramp all remain aligned to the same moving car. Check rotated head-on hostile bodywork, oncoming approach velocity, attack-window fairness, civilian separation and fork escape corridors. Automated Chromium captures and unit tests check these contracts; real-hardware playtesting and manual collision-balance approval remain outstanding.

## Low / High manual-gear acceptance (2026-10-21)

At game start, verify the HUD reads **LOW**; hold W and confirm the interceptor accelerates quickly but levels off at the lower speed ceiling. Tap G once (or mapped gamepad right shoulder) to engage **HIGH**; with throttle held the car should pull toward the top of the viewport and reach a higher speed. Verify High is sluggish from low initial speed, Low downshifts decelerate smoothly, a held shift key does not rapidly oscillate, and key/button reassignment persists after reload while rejecting duplicates. Test keyboard plus at least two real gamepad models. Confirm entering a boathouse resets the display to **BOAT**, water controls ignore shifts, and redeployment begins back in Low. New browser tests exercise input and HUD under software WebGL; real-hardware feel and original 1983 transmission curve still need signoff.

## Rear-pursuit and signed relative traffic validation (2026-10-21)

During road combat beyond 550 distance units, check that tail cars appear infrequently from behind, clearly telegraphed in the tactical HUD, and do not appear while the weapons van is deployed, on water or inside divided-road hazards. Low gear and braking should allow faster rear traffic to approach; switching to High and driving away should leave tail cars behind and eventually award **TAIL SHAKEN +125**, unless scoring is suppressed. Steering out of the target lane and smoke/oil should provide tactical counterplay without instantaneous homing. Ensure no two simultaneous tail cars, no overlapping spawn traffic, no surprise rear collision while docking, and no lingering offscreen traffic after sustained braking. Verify ordinary civilian overtakes and emergency braking remain fair, and head-on enemies still approach with additive relative closing speed. CI covers deterministic unit and Chromium checks; target-device and high/low mode play sessions remain outstanding.
